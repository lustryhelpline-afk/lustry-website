08 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Need something specific? We're happy to discuss your product, quantity or custom design.

Talk to Lustry ↗
What is the minimum order for custom manufacturing?

Bulk custom manufacturing enquiries start from 25 pieces. Selected retail products may be available from one piece after inventory confirmation.

Can I choose fabric, GSM and printing?

Yes. The manufacturing form includes product, fabric, GSM where applicable, colour, branding and quantity. Final feasibility and pricing are confirmed by the team.

Can I see an estimated price online?

Where configured product pricing is available, the estimator shows an indicative amount. Missing specifications require a team-reviewed quotation.

Can I upload my own artwork?

Artwork and logo requirements can be described in the enquiry. Secure file upload and AI mockups are planned features, not live production tools.
