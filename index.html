Frequently asked

A few useful details.

Need help with product selection or material specifications? Send a brief and our team can guide you.

What is the minimum order quantity?

The stated minimum order quantity is 25 pieces. Some product types, materials or customisations may require a different minimum, which will be confirmed in the quote.

Is the calculator price final?

No. Any estimate shown is illustrative only. Final pricing depends on the product, material, GSM, quantity, artwork, branding, packaging and delivery requirements.

Can I use my own logo or design?

Yes, share your artwork or brand guidelines during the enquiry. File formats and production suitability can be confirmed with the team.

Can you help me choose a fabric?

Yes. Tell us how the product will be used, the feel you want and your budget range. The team can recommend suitable material options.
