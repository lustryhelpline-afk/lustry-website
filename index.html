GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Need help with a specification? Ask the Lustry support assistant or send us a quote request.

What is the minimum order quantity?

The current website setup uses a minimum request quantity of 25 pieces. Special products may require a higher MOQ; Lustry will confirm this in the quotation.

Are the prices final?

No. The website shows an indicative estimate. The final price is confirmed by Lustry after checking the product specification, artwork, size mix, taxes, shipping and timeline.

Can I order different sizes?

Yes. For sized products, use the size breakdown. If you enter sizes, their total must match the total quantity.

How do coupons and gifts work?

Eligible customers may receive a coupon after a qualifying completed order. Coupon validity, minimum order value and available gifts must be confirmed by Lustry. This starter version does not automatically grant rewards until the reward rules are configured in the backend.

When does production start?

Production should begin only after the final quotation, artwork, specifications, timeline and payment terms are approved by both sides.
