GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked.

What is your minimum bulk order?

Our custom manufacturing minimum order quantity is 25 pieces. Selected retail products will support single-piece orders when verified stock and checkout are enabled.

Are website estimates final?

No. Estimates are indicative; Lustry must confirm your final price and production requirements.

Can I upload my logo?

You can preview a logo locally in our Design Studio and carry the text brief into your enquiry. Files are not uploaded or attached; coordinate the original artwork with our team after submission.

Is online retail checkout available?

Not yet in this preview. The cart is for demonstration only and no payment can be made.
